What is creatine?
A compound your body already makes and that you also get from meat and fish. It is one of the most researched sports supplements.
What does it do?
It helps your muscles regenerate ATP, the energy used in short, intense efforts like lifting and sprinting.
How do I use it?
Most people take 3–5 g a day with water, every day. Consistency matters more than timing. Drink enough water.
